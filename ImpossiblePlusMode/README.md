# ImpossiblePlusMode

This mod significantly enhances the difficulty of the "Impossible" difficulty mode in *Guns, Gore and Cannoli 2*. Enemies are more aggressive, more durable, and appear in greater numbers, delivering a truly punishing experience for veteran players seeking the ultimate challenge.

## ✨ Major Changes Overview

### 🌍 Global Adjustments
- Enemies in "Impossible" difficulty now have an even higher chance of spawning additional reinforcements, making every encounter more chaotic and intense.

### 🔫 Ranged Enemies
- **Revolver Gangsters**: Increased bullet damage, added knockback and penetration capabilities, adjusted attack timing, faster projectile speed, and replaced firing sound effects.
- **Revolver Riot Shield Police**: Faster projectile speed for more threatening ranged attacks.
- **Mauser German Soldiers**: Added penetration capability (able to hit multiple targets) and significantly increased projectile speed.
- **Thompson Gangsters / Thompson Police**: Extended attack duration with slightly shorter attack intervals, making their sustained fire more dangerous.
- **Schmeisser German Soldiers**: Adjusted attack cycle timing for more unpredictable burst patterns.
- **AK47 German Soldiers**: Extended attack duration with shorter attack intervals, increasing overall DPS.
- **Shotgun US Soldiers / German Soldiers**: Increased damage and knockback, with significantly shorter attack intervals for faster follow-up shots.
- **US Riflemen / German Riflemen**: Shortened attack intervals and faster projectile speed, making them more dangerous at range.

### 💥 Special Enemy Replacements
- **Flamethrower German Soldiers**: Now have a small chance to appear in place of Shotgun German Soldiers.
- **Rocket Launcher German Soldiers (Tracking Missiles)**: Now have a small chance to appear in place of standard Rocket Launcher German Soldiers.

### 🤖 Mechanical Units
- Significantly increased health pools for all German turrets and boss units throughout the game, making these high-priority threats much more durable and demanding more focused firepower to take down.

### 🥊 Melee Enemies
- **Bench-wielding Gangsters**: Increased bench swing damage.
- **Wrench-wielding Gangsters**: Increased wrench strike damage.
- **Riot Police**: Increased baton damage.
- **German Guards**: Increased blade slash damage.
- **All Enemies' Kick Attacks**: Increased knockback, making their melee counters more disruptive.
- **Bodyguards**: Increased charging attack knockback, making their rushes harder to withstand.

### 🧟 Zombies & Mutants
- **Brute Zombies**: Increased punch damage and knockback, making them even more devastating in melee.
- **Mechanical Brutes**: Increased slash damage and knockback, posing a greater threat up close.
- **Armless Zombies / Maid Zombies**: Increased swipe damage, making even basic zombies more dangerous.
- **Small Rats**: Increased pounce damage, and now have a small chance to appear in place of Prisoner Zombies in early-game levels.
- **Medium Rats**: Now have a small chance to appear in place of Runner Zombies in early-game levels.
- **Large Rats**: Now have a small chance to appear in place of Soldier Zombies in early-game levels.

## ⚙️ Installation

Place the compiled `.dll` file of this mod into the `BepInEx/plugins` folder within your game directory.  
For more detailed installation steps, please refer to the [main README](../../README.md).

## 🔗 Dependencies

- **BepInEx 5.4.23.5** or later ([Download](https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx/releases/tag/v5.4.23.5))
- **.NET Framework 4.7.2** (runtime environment)

## 🎮 Compatibility

- This mod is built for game version **release_1.0.8** and is designed to modify enemy AI and stats within the "Impossible" difficulty.
- May conflict with other mods that alter enemy behavior, spawn rates, or stats. Use with caution.

# 噩梦难度模式 (ImpossiblePlusMode)

本模组大幅提升了《枪血意大利黑手党2》中“极难”(Impossible)难度下的敌人强度。敌人更加凶猛、更难对付，且数量更多，为追求终极挑战的老玩家带来真正的压迫感。

## ✨ 主要改动一览

### 🌍 全局调整
- “极难”难度下敌人额外刷新的概率进一步提升，让每一场战斗都更加混乱激烈。

### 🔫 远程敌人
- **左轮黑帮**：子弹伤害提升，增加击退与穿透能力，攻击节奏调整，弹速加快，并替换了开火音效。
- **左轮防爆盾特警**：子弹飞行速度提升，远程威胁更大。
- **毛瑟德军**：增加穿透能力（可穿透多个目标），子弹飞行速度显著提升。
- **汤姆逊黑帮 / 汤姆逊特警**：攻击持续时间延长，攻击间隔略微缩短，持续火力更具压制力。
- **施迈瑟冲锋枪德军**：攻击循环节奏调整，射击模式更难以预测。
- **AK47 步枪德军**：攻击持续时间延长，攻击间隔缩短，整体输出效率提高。
- **霰弹枪美军 / 霰弹枪德军**：伤害与击退提升，攻击间隔大幅缩短，连发速度更快。
- **美军步枪手 / 德军步枪手**：攻击间隔缩短，子弹飞行速度提升，远程威胁更大。

### 💥 特殊敌人替换
- **喷火器德军**：现在有小概率替代霰弹枪德军出现。
- **跟踪导弹德军**（火箭筒德军变种）：现在有小概率替代普通火箭筒德军出现。

### 💀 BOSS 与炮台
- 大幅增加了游戏中所有德军炮台和 BOSS 单位的生命值，使这些高优先级威胁更加耐打，需要玩家集中火力才能有效击破。

### 🥊 近战敌人
- **板凳黑帮**：板凳挥击伤害提升。
- **扳手黑帮**：扳手打击伤害提升。
- **防暴特警**：警棍伤害提升。
- **德军警卫**：挥刀伤害提升。
- **所有敌人踢击**：击退效果增强，近身反击更具威胁。
- **保镖**：冲锋撞击的击退效果增强，冲撞更难抵御。

### 🧟 僵尸与变异体
- **野兽僵尸**：拳击伤害与击退提升，近战破坏力更强。
- **机械野兽**：挥刀伤害与击退提升，近身威胁更大。
- **断臂僵尸 / 女仆僵尸**：挥击伤害提升，基础僵尸也更加危险。
- **小型老鼠**：飞扑伤害提升，并在游戏前期关卡中有小概率替代囚犯僵尸出现。
- **中型老鼠**：在游戏前期关卡中有小概率替代跑尸出现。
- **大型老鼠**：在游戏前期关卡中有小概率替代士兵僵尸出现。
