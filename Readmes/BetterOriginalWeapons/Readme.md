# BetterOriginalWeapons

This mod systematically rebalances and refines all original weapons in *Guns, Gore and Cannoli 2*, aiming to make each weapon more distinctive and practical while enhancing the overall combat fluidity and enjoyment.

## ✨ Major Changes Overview

### 🎯 Automatic Weapons
- **Thompson Submachine Gun**: Increased damage, significantly larger magazine and reserve ammo, greatly improved endurance.
- **Schmeisser Submachine Gun**: Slightly increased damage, more reserve ammo for sustained suppression.
- **MG42 Machine Gun**: Increased damage, optimized fire rate, enhanced knockback effect, more reserve ammo for heavier suppressive fire.
- **AK47 Rifle**: Increased damage and magazine capacity, more reserve ammo, making mid-to-long-range engagements more comfortable.

### 🔫 Pistols
- **Standard Pistol**: Increased damage and magazine capacity, more useful in early game.
- **Dual Pistols**: Increased damage, magazine size, and reserve ammo, offering heavier dual-wielding firepower.
- **Mauser Pistol**: Significantly increased damage, improved penetration (able to pierce more enemies), faster projectile speed, smoother handling.

### ⚔️ Melee Weapons
- **Kick**: Increased damage and knockback, more effective for close-quarters self-defense.
- **Baseball Bat**: Significantly increased damage, stronger melee impact.
- **Chainsaw**: Greatly increased damage and fuel capacity, better sustained combat capability.

### 💣 Explosive Weapons
- **Rocket Launcher**: Slightly larger blast radius, increased damage and reserve ammo, more effective for crowd control.
- **Grenade Launcher**: Increased damage, more threatening against groups of enemies.
- **88mm Cannon Shell**: Increased carrying capacity, allowing more frequent use of heavy firepower.

### 🎯 Shotguns & Flamethrower
- **Shotgun**: Increased damage, knockback, magazine capacity, and reserve ammo, greater close-range burst potential.
- **Flamethrower**: Increased base damage, making continuous burning more lethal.

## ⚙️ Installation

Place the compiled `.dll` file of this mod into the `BepInEx/plugins` folder within your game directory.  
For more detailed installation steps, please refer to the [main README](../../README.md).

## 🔗 Dependencies

- **BepInEx 5.4.23.5** or later ([Download](https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx/releases/tag/v5.4.23.5))
- **.NET Framework 4.7.2** (runtime environment)

## 🎮 Compatibility

- This mod is built for game version **release_1.0.8**. It is compatible with other mods that do not modify original weapon stats.
- If used alongside other mods that alter weapon properties, conflicts may occur. Please use with caution.

# 更好的原版武器 (BetterOriginalWeapons)

本模组对《枪血意大利黑手党2》中的全部原版武器进行了系统性的平衡调整与手感优化，旨在让每一把武器都更具特色和实用价值，同时提升战斗的流畅度和爽快感。

## ✨ 主要改动一览

### 🎯 全自动武器
- **汤姆逊冲锋枪**：伤害提升，弹匣容量与备弹量大幅增加，续航能力显著增强。
- **施迈瑟冲锋枪**：伤害小幅上调，备弹量更加充裕，适合持久压制。
- **MG42 机枪**：伤害提高，射速得到优化，击退效果增强，同时备弹量增加，火力压制感更强。
- **AK47 步枪**：伤害与弹匣容量均有所提升，备弹量增加，让中远距离交火更加从容。

### 🔫 手枪系
- **普通手枪**：伤害与弹匣容量提高，初期更实用。
- **双手枪**：伤害、弹匣容量和备弹量全面提升，双枪火力更猛。
- **毛瑟枪**：伤害大幅提升，穿透能力增强（可穿透更多敌人），子弹飞行速度加快，手感更加利落。

### ⚔️ 近战武器
- **踢击**：伤害与击退效果加强，近身自卫更有效。
- **球棍**：伤害显著提高，近战打击感更强。
- **电锯**：伤害大幅提升，燃料容量增加，持续作战能力增强。

### 💣 爆炸类武器
- **火箭筒**：爆炸范围略微扩大，伤害与备弹量提高，清场能力更优秀。
- **榴弹发射器**：伤害提升，对群体敌人威胁更大。
- **88 炮炮弹**：携带量增加，可更频繁地使用重型火力。

### 🎯 霰弹枪与火焰喷射器
- **霰弹枪**：伤害、击退效果、弹匣容量及备弹量均得到增强，近战爆发力更足。
- **火焰喷射器**：基础伤害提高，持续灼烧效果更具威胁。

## ⚙️ 安装方法

请将本模组编译后的 `.dll` 文件放置于游戏目录下的 `BepInEx/plugins` 文件夹中。  
更详细的安装步骤请参考 [主 README](../../README.md) 中的说明。
